# Simmer music

The built-in tracks (Sunday morning, Freshly picked, and Slow simmer) are original procedural compositions generated by `lib/music.ts`. All tones and percussion are synthesized from mathematical waveforms and deterministic noise. They contain no third-party recordings, samples, or copied melodies.
